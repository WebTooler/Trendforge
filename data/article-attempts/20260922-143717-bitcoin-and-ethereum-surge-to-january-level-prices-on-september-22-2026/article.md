## Morning rally pushes prices to January highs
The cryptocurrency market opened higher on Tuesday, September 22, 2026, with Bitcoin and Ethereum both posting gains of more than 10 % over the previous week and month. This morning’s opening prices for the two leading digital assets exceeded the levels last reached in January of the same year, marking a notable surge for the market.

## Short liquidations fuel the climb
A significant factor behind the rally is the rise in short‑position liquidations. CoinGlass reports that liquidations over the past 24 hours totalled $602 million, with Ethereum contributing $187 million compared to $184 million for Bitcoin. In the latest hour alone, liquidations reached $634.62 million, about 84 % of which stemmed from short positions. These figures suggest that traders covering shorts are pushing prices upward.

## Market context and ongoing volatility
Bitcoin’s price has swung sharply in recent months, and the current move represents a continuation of that volatility. While the surge shows strong daily momentum, the broader market remains sensitive to liquidity changes and short‑position dynamics.

## What to watch next
Investors should monitor short‑position activity and liquidity levels, as they are closely tied to price swings. Additionally, the evolving regulatory and technological landscape for cryptocurrencies may impact future price movements, although no specific changes are documented in the current data.

## Takeaway
The day’s price gains for Bitcoin and Ethereum reflect a combination of weekly performance gains and a sharp increase in short‑position liquidations. The surge to January‑level prices underscores the market’s sensitivity to liquidity dynamics, but the underlying volatility remains high. Observing short‑position trends and liquidity flows will be essential for understanding future price trajectories.
