## Market snapshot
On Wednesday, September 23, 2026, Bitcoin and Ethereum opened close to their highest levels in eight months. The price of Bitcoin remained flat compared to Tuesday’s opening, indicating a pause after recent gains. The week’s earlier moves saw Bitcoin rise more than 5% on Friday and another 6% on Monday, giving the broader market a short‑term lift.

## Signals of a shifting sentiment
Sean Farrell, head of digital assets at Fundstrat, told Yahoo Finance that “the crypto winter is over, although that does not necessarily mean the path higher will be linear.” His comment reflects a broader observation that both Bitcoin and Ethereum are trading near peaks that were only reached eight months ago, coinciding with political headlines about President Trump’s upcoming visit to China.

## Market volatility and tax considerations
Despite the recent rally, volatility remains. XRP fell 15% over the previous 24 hours, and Bitcoin fell below $67,000 on Thursday before trading just above $68,000. The global crypto market capitalization dropped 7.4% on that day to $2.37 trillion, down from a peak above $4.2 trillion in September 2025.

Converting Bitcoin into Ethereum is not treated as a simple trade by the IRS. The transaction triggers a taxable event, and any gains realized in 2025 must be reported on the 2025 tax return filed in early 2026.

## Long‑term outlook for major coins
Bitcoin continues to attract momentum, with ETF inflows suggesting that investors expect further upside. Ethereum offers a carry component through staking yields of approximately 3–4% annually, a feature that many blue‑chip assets lack. Analysts anticipate that profits moving out of Bitcoin may flow into the Ethereum ecosystem.

Investors looking for long‑term exposure should consider assets with large user bases and high trading volumes, such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, rather than newer altcoins that may experience sharper swings.

## Practical next steps for investors
1. **Stay informed about tax reporting**: Crypto gains from 2025 will appear on the 2025 return, so maintain accurate records of all transactions.
2. **Monitor market cap trends**: A 7.4% daily drop in global market capitalization indicates broader risk.
3. **Consider staking options**: Ethereum’s 3–4% annual yields provide a modest income stream.
4. **Avoid assuming linear growth**: Even with recent gains, the path ahead may not be straight.

## What the evidence shows
- Bitcoin and Ethereum opened near eight‑month highs on September 23, 2026.
- The crypto winter may be ending, though gains are not guaranteed to be linear.
- Converting Bitcoin to Ethereum triggers a taxable event reported on the relevant tax return.

These facts underscore that while the market exhibits fresh momentum, volatility and tax rules continue to shape the investment landscape.
