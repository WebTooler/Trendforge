## A Record Rally After a Policy Shock
Bitcoin’s price jumped 14% to about $86,000 after the U.S. Congress failed to pass the CLARITY Act and the Federal Reserve increased rates, closing above its 50‑week moving average for the first time in almost a year. The move coincided with a one‑day inflow of $999 million into U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs, the largest daily influx since October 2025. The surge pushed Bitcoin above $85,000 for the first time in nearly a year, breaking a key resistance level that had held since the beginning of the year.

## Momentum and Investor Confidence
Jesse Marre, a senior portfolio manager at Hilbert Group, said the vote and rate hike were already priced in, which explains why Bitcoin briefly dipped below $75,000 before rallying again. The dip and rebound demonstrate that buyers stepped in to push the price above $75,000, a level that had been tested multiple times earlier in the month. The rally also attracted record ETF inflows, indicating institutional demand that may sustain the upward trajectory.

## Short‑Term Volatility
During the same week, the yield on 10‑year Treasury notes rose to as high as 5.01% before falling to 4.94% on September 17, then rebounding again. This volatility in bond markets contributed to a sharp rally on September 21, when Bitcoin hit a high of $87,283 but closed the day at $84,378. The price then retraced to around $84,700 by September 25, giving the market a 4.7% weekly gain.

## Is the Peak a New Trend?
Analysts are debating whether the September 21 peak represents the start of a new bull market or merely a short‑term spike. Marre views the strong follow‑through as the beginning of a new bull run, while Chait notes that reclaiming the 50‑week average marks a significant shift in trend. However, the price has already retraced below $85,000, and many market participants remain uncertain about the sustainability of the rally.

## Key Levels to Watch
Support around $75,000 has proven resilient, as buyers stepped in after brief dips. Resistance at $83,000 and $85,000 was breached during the rally, but the subsequent pullback suggests those levels may still exert influence. If the 10‑year interest rate rises above 5.01% again, the recent 14% gain could be reversed before the price reaches $95,000. Traders should also monitor ETF inflow trends and the 50‑week moving average as potential indicators of longer‑term momentum.

## Takeaway
Bitcoin’s rise to an eight‑month high and subsequent retreat below $85,000 illustrates how regulatory outcomes and monetary policy can quickly reshape market dynamics. Record ETF inflows and a breach of key technical levels signal strong demand, but recent volatility in bond yields and a rapid pullback underline the short‑term uncertainty. Investors should track ETF cash flows, Treasury yields, and the 50‑week moving average to gauge whether the current rally signals a lasting trend or a temporary correction.
