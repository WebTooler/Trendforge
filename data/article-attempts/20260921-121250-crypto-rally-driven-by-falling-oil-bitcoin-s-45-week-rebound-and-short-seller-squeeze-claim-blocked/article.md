## Market Momentum
The crypto market is rallying, a trend that has been sharpened by a fourth consecutive day of lower oil prices. The decline in energy costs helped push the 10‑year Treasury yield below 5%.

## Bitcoin’s Key Breakout
Bitcoin closed the week above a level it hadn’t held in 45 weeks. When that price milestone was breached, about $648 million in short positions were forced to close. 

## Short‑Seller Pressure and XRP
Funding rates for XRP perpetual contracts had been negative for more than a week. Once XRP’s price began to rise, the short side faced mounting pressure, contributing to a 6.4% gain for XRP versus a 5.3% gain for Bitcoin.

## Liquidity Shocks
Within an hour of Bitcoin passing the 45‑week line, exchanges liquidated roughly $262.3 million in shorts, according to CoinGlass. About $218.55 million of that came from Bitcoin shorts, while about $9.53 million came from long positions.

## Limited ETF and Regulatory Impact
ETF inflows for the week ending September 19 were $6.21 million. 

## Historical Context and Uncertainty
  However, Bitcoin last closed above this line on November 9, 2025, and has spent 44 weeks below it since, indicating that the current surge could be transient.

## Take‑away
The recent surge is driven by a drop in oil prices, a strong weekly close for Bitcoin, and a surge in short sellers. ETF inflows were $6.21 million for the week ending September 19.
