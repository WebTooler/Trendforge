## Opening Prices Near 8‑Month Highs
On the morning of Wednesday, September 23, 2026, both Bitcoin and Ethereum began trading close to their 8‑month highs. The surge follows a sequence of weekly gains, with Bitcoin jumping more than 5 % on Friday and another 6 % on Monday, according to recent market data. The rally coincides with political headlines, as President Trump prepares to host Chinese President Xi Jinping in Washington this week.

## Recent Market Movements
Bitcoin’s price has been volatile, swinging sharply in recent months. A notable rise of over 5 % on Friday and 6 % on Monday contributed to the current near‑peak opening levels. In contrast, other cryptocurrencies have shown weaker performance; for instance, XRP fell 15 % in the last 24 hours as markets swung bearish, and Bitcoin briefly dipped below $67,000 before trading just above $68,000.

## Tax Considerations for Conversions
Converting Bitcoin into Ethereum is not treated as a simple trade by the IRS. The tax code requires such activity to be reported on the taxpayer’s return for the year in which the conversion occurred. Thus, if an investor sold crypto for profit in 2025, that transaction would be reported on the 2025 return filed in early 2026.

## Investor Sentiment and Asset Selection
For investors entering the market now, the focus is less on whether to buy crypto and more on selecting assets that align with individual time horizons and risk appetites. Bitcoin currently shows strong momentum and is leading the charge, with positive ETF inflows suggesting potential room for further upside. Ethereum, with staking yields around 3–4 % annually, offers a carry component that many other large‑cap assets lack, and profits are rotating into the Ethereum ecosystem.

## Current Market Cap Context
The global cryptocurrency market capitalization has fallen 7.4 % to $2.37 trillion after peaking above $4.2 trillion in September 2025. This decline reflects broader bearish sentiment across several altcoins, even as Bitcoin and Ethereum see temporary gains.

## What to Watch Next
Readers should monitor how the upward momentum sustains itself beyond the recent weekly gains. The path higher is unlikely to be linear, and the broader market’s volatility remains a key risk factor. Additionally, tax reporting deadlines for 2025 activity in early 2026 mean investors will need to reconcile their portfolios accordingly.

## Key Takeaways
- Bitcoin and Ethereum opened near 8‑month highs on September 23, 2026, after a series of weekly gains.
