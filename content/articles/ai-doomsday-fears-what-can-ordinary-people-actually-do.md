## The hype versus the facts
 The Guardian and other outlets have hosted debates in which tech reporters Aisha Down, Blake Montgomery and Dan Milmo answer readers’ questions about the reality of an AI‑driven doomsday.
The reporters note that many of the scenarios—nuclear war, societal collapse or bioweapons—sound “too wooly to really take seriously.” They point out that the challenges of creating new deadly viruses are largely administrative, not technical, and that historical attempts such as Aum Shinrikyo’s botulism and anthrax spray have failed.

## What would an AI apocalypse look like?
 It seems unlikely that AI at this point can significantly change the equation around bioweapons or viruses. One researcher, Jacy Reese Anthis, has highlighted the “staggering gulf between how biological brains evolved and how AI systems are built.”

## Why regulators and journalists are hesitant
The Guardian interview highlights a perception that tech CEOs are shocked when the public’s opinion is negative.
