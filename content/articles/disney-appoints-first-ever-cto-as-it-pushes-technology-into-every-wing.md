## A New Tech Flagship at Disney
Walt Disney Co. announced that Karandeep Anand will become the company’s first chief technology officer, a position that did not exist in Disney’s C‑suite before. Anand will report directly to CEO Josh D’Amaro and will oversee enterprise technology, infrastructure, data and artificial intelligence platforms, as well as product and engineering across the company. The role was created in October and is intended to modernise how Disney builds and delivers technology company‑wide.

## Anand’s Background and the Rationale
Walt Disney Co. has named Karandeep Anand to the newly created role of chief technology officer, marking the first time the entertainment giant will have a corporate‑wide technology chief. He was chief executive officer of Character.AI, an online chatbot platform, and before that served as president and chief product officer at Brex and held vice‑presidential roles at Meta’s Facebook and Microsoft. He holds a Computer Science degree from the International Institute of Information Technology in Hyderabad. Disney’s CEO, Josh D’Amaro, has highlighted technology as a priority since taking the helm, particularly in expanding digital offerings. D’Amaro’s prior role as head of theme parks and cruises involved investment in Epic Games, and he has outlined plans to broaden Disney+ beyond movies and television into gaming and other experiences.

## What the Move Signals for Disney’s Strategy
D’Amaro has focused on maintaining Disney's momentum in core growth areas such as streaming and parks. D’Amaro has repeatedly emphasized the need to keep Disney’s storytelling at the forefront while using technology to enhance creativity and operate as “One Disney.” With the new CTO, Disney aims to deepen its direct relationship with fans, turning Disney+ into a hub that combines streaming, gaming, shopping and other services. The company has floated the idea of a free, ad‑supported tier for Disney+ as a “front porch” to attract more users and has hinted at integrating the platform with its parks, cruises and merchandise.
